Return window

Eligible unopened and unused products may be requested for return within 30 days of delivery. The original packaging, labels and seals must remain intact.

×

Items we cannot accept

Opened, used, partly consumed, tampered or improperly stored products cannot be returned. Clearance items and products marked non-returnable are also excluded.

!

Damaged or incorrect order

Submit the Order ID and clear photos of the product, outer package and shipping label. Keep all packaging until the case is resolved.

⇄

Exchanges

Approved exchanges depend on stock availability. If a replacement is unavailable, the team may offer an eligible refund instead.

How to request a return

  1. Open the dedicated Return Request page.
  2. Enter your Order ID, select a reason and describe the issue.
  3. Verify your phone number and attach clear supporting images.
  4. Submit the request and wait for approval and collection or return instructions.
RefundsApproved refunds are sent to the original online payment method after the returned item passes inspection. Bank or payment-provider processing times may vary.
