✓

When is an order confirmed?

Your order is confirmed only after the payment gateway verifies a successful payment. You will then receive an order confirmation and payment reference.

↻

Payment failed or pending?

Do not retry repeatedly while a payment is pending. Check your bank account and order history first. If money was debited without an order confirmation, contact support with the payment reference.

▤

GST invoice

Add the correct billing and GST details during checkout when required. Documents become available after successful payment verification.

◈

Payment safety

Card and UPI credentials are entered in the secure Razorpay checkout. Clearly does not ask you to share card PINs, UPI PINs or OTPs with support staff.

Having trouble with a payment?

Share your payment reference—never your PIN or OTP—with support.
