DELIVERY GUIDE

Shipping made clear.

From careful packing to doorstep delivery, here is what to expect after placing your Clearly order.

🚚
⌛

Dispatch time

Paid orders are normally prepared and dispatched within 24–48 hours. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays begin processing on the next working day.

₹

Delivery charges

Shipping is free on orders of ₹499 or more. A ₹59 delivery charge applies below that value and is displayed before payment.

⌖

Delivery estimate

Delivery time depends on the PIN code, courier coverage and product availability. Use the delivery checker on the product page for the estimate available to your location.

▣

Safe handling

Products are checked and packed to reduce movement and leakage in transit. Larger or chemical-product orders may arrive in separate packages.

✓

After dispatch

  • Keep your phone available for courier delivery updates.
  • Use your Order ID on the Track your order page to see the latest available status.
  • Address changes may not be possible after dispatch.
  • If a package arrives visibly damaged or leaking, photograph it before opening and contact support promptly.
